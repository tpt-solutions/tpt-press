# Code of Conduct

Participants in tpt-press are expected to be respectful, constructive and
considerate. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others'
private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or any other
project space.

Maintainers at TPT Solutions may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
and other contributions that do not follow these expectations, and may restrict
participants who repeatedly violate them.

Report concerns privately to TPT Solutions through the repository's GitHub
security reporting form (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
